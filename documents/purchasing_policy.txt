
SUPPLY CHAIN PURCHASING POLICY

1. Reordering:
Products should be reviewed for reordering when stock reaches or falls below the reorder level.

2. Critical stock:
If estimated days of stock remaining is less than or equal to supplier delivery time, treat the product as critical.

3. Supplier selection:
Compare supplier delivery time, price, product quality, and reliability before choosing a supplier.

4. Safety stock:
Use 15 days of average sales as the sample safety-stock target for this project.

5. Purchase approval:
AI-generated order suggestions must be reviewed by an authorized employee before an order is placed.

6. Data accuracy:
If information is missing, state that it is unknown rather than inventing facts.
